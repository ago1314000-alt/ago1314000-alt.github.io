The Adventurer's First Quest

A one-page reference for your very first game of Dungeons & Dragons
The Golden Rule
Describe what you want to do. The DM tells you what to roll. That is the whole game.
Know these three numbers on your sheet
ACArmor ClassHow hard you are to hit
HPHit PointsYour health: at 0, you fall
SPDSpeedHow far you move each turn
At Any Time

Ability Checks

The DM says "make a Perception check." Roll the d20, add the number next to that skill on your sheet. Higher is better. A natural 20 is glorious; a natural 1 is trouble.

Saving Throws

Same deal, but reacting to danger: dodging a trap, resisting a spell. d20 + the save number. The DM will tell you which one.

Advantage & Disadvantage

Advantage: roll two d20s, keep the higher.
Disadvantage: roll two, keep the lower.

Spellcasters

Your spells say what to roll. When in doubt, read the spell aloud; the DM will sort it out.

In Combat

Initiative

Combat starts? Roll d20 + your Initiative number. That sets the turn order.

Your Turn, in Three Steps

  1. MOVE: up to your Speed, before, after, or split around your action.
  2. ACTION: pick one: Attack · Cast a Spell · Dash · Hide · or try something creative, just ask!
  3. BONUS ACTION: only if your sheet says you have one.

Making an Attack

Roll d20 + attack bonus vs. their AC. If you meet or beat it, roll your damage die. Done.

At 0 Hit Points

You fall unconscious, not dead. The DM will walk you through what happens next.

The Dice of Fate
The d20 does 90% of the work; the rest are mostly for damage. Your DM will always say which to roll. (Click a die to roll it!)
You Cannot Play Wrong
Ask questions anytime. Every player at the table was new once. It is always okay to say: "I'm not sure what to do; what are my options?"
Everything else, the Dungeon Master handles. Your job is to have fun. Roll for adventure!
