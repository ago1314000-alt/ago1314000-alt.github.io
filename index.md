## ✨ What Is moreland?

moreland transforms your Android tablet into a **wired second monitor** for your Linux computer. Instead of buying an expensive portable display, you can use the tablet you already own to expand your workspace. The connection happens over a simple USB cable - no Wi-Fi, no network setup, no complicated configuration.

Think of it as extending your computer's desktop onto your tablet screen, just like connecting a regular monitor - but using the USB port instead of HDMI.

## 🖥️ How It Works

Your computer sends the display signal through a USB cable directly to your tablet. The technology behind it is incredibly efficient - it uses **hardware encoding** on your computer's graphics processor and **zero-copy** technology to ensure there's no lag. This means:

- **Instant response** - move your mouse and watch it appear on your tablet immediately
- **Crystal clear picture** - full resolution with no compression artifacts
- **No network interference** - the USB connection is dedicated and stable

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Tablet not detected?**
- Make sure USB debugging is enabled in Developer Options
- Try a different USB cable or port
- Unlock your tablet while connecting

**Display is blank or flickering?**
- Disconnect and reconnect the USB cable
- Check that your GPU drivers are up to date
- Restart both your computer and tablet

**Performance is slow?**
- Use a USB 3.0 port for faster data transfer
- Close unnecessary apps on your tablet
- Reduce the display resolution in moreland settings
